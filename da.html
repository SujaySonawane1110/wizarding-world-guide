DUMBLEDORE'S ARMY

The secret student resistance group formed to learn practical defensive magic and oppose dark forces.

THE RESISTANCE BEGINS

Formed in secret under the nose of Dolores Umbridge, Dumbledore's Army was a group of Hogwarts students dedicated to training in Defense Against the Dark Arts. Under Harry Potter's instruction, they learned to cast Patronuses and combat curses, eventually playing a pivotal role in the Battle of Hogwarts.

CHARACTERS OF THE GROUP

NEVILLE LONGBOTTOM

Neville Longbottom's journey from clumsy, accident-prone student to courageous warrior stands as one of the series' most profound character arcs. Haunted by his parents' torture-induced incapacity and their absence from his life, Neville initially struggled with confidence and magical ability. Yet beneath his awkwardness lay genuine bravery and moral strength that gradually emerged through his participation in Dumbledore's Army.

Under Harry's instruction, Neville discovered his magical potential and developed genuine friendship and camaraderie. His ultimate courage manifested in the Battle of Hogwarts when he destroyed the final Horcrux, Nagini, an act that may have been more crucial than any other single moment in the final battle. His heroic sacrifice of his own fears and limitations transformed him into the truest embodiment of Gryffindor values.

HOUSE: Gryffindor WAND: 13", Cherry, Unicorn Hair PATRONUS: Non-corporeal

GINNY WEASLEY

Ginny Weasley was the youngest Weasley and a natural athlete with impressive magical talent. Possessed by Tom Riddle's memory during her first year, she barely survived the possession. This traumatic experience forged her into a stronger, more resilient person. Her mastery of the Bat-Bogey Hex and her fearless attitude in combat made her one of the most formidable young witches of her generation.

As the only female co-leader of Dumbledore's Army alongside Harry, Ginny demonstrated both tactical thinking and combat prowess. In the Battle of Hogwarts, she fought alongside the most experienced warriors. Her ultimate union with Harry Potter symbolized the healing and hope possible after trauma and war, representing a future beyond conflict.

HOUSE: Gryffindor WAND: Yew, Unknown core PATRONUS: Horse

LUNA LOVEGOOD

Luna Lovegood was an eccentric Ravenclaw whose belief in creatures and phenomena that most wizards dismissed as mythical revealed either profound magical insight or delightful imagination. Despite being openly mocked for her unconventional beliefs and her father's controversial publication, Luna maintained her individuality and genuine kindness toward all she encountered. Her perceptiveness and ability to see people as they truly were made her an invaluable friend.

Luna's participation in Dumbledore's Army represented her desire to fight genuine evil while maintaining her unique perspective. Captured by Death Eaters and imprisoned alongside other captives, her survival and recovery post-war demonstrated her resilience. Her continued friendship with the core trio proved that genuine connection transcended social convention and peer judgment.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw WAND: Unknown PATRONUS: Hare

FRED & GEORGE WEASLEY

Fred and George Weasley were identical twins whose magical innovation and sense of humor masked genuine intelligence and courage. Their talent for magical invention and their willingness to stand against authority through pranks and mischief made them beloved across Hogwarts. Their participation in Dumbledore's Army, though often cloaked in humor, revealed their serious commitment to fighting Dark forces.

Fred's death in the Battle of Hogwarts struck George deeply, leaving him permanently bereft of his other half. Their successful establishment of Weasleys' Wizard Wheezes demonstrated their entrepreneurial genius and their refusal to abandon joy and humor even during dark times. Fred's loss symbolized the casual brutality of war that struck even the most vibrant and talented.

HOUSE: Gryffindor SKILL: Magical Invention PATRONUS: Magpie

CHO CHANG

Cho Chang was the popular and talented Ravenclaw Seeker whose beauty and athletic prowess made her one of the most admired students at Hogwarts. Her relationship with Cedric Diggory, and its tragic conclusion with his death, left her emotionally vulnerable. Her subsequent romantic interest in Harry was complicated by her grief and her eventual paranoia regarding betrayal.

Cho's participation in Dumbledore's Army represented her attempts to process her trauma through action. Her eventual path diverged from the core group, yet her early membership demonstrated her willingness to resist authority when conscience demanded. Her survival of the war allowed her to eventually heal from her losses.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw WAND: Unknown PATRONUS: Swan

DEAN THOMAS

Dean Thomas was an artistic and good-natured Gryffindor whose genuine interest in learning defensive magic made him an ideal DA member. His artistic talent and creative approach to spell-work contributed valuable perspective to group training. His warm personality and consistent loyalty to Harry made him a reliable member of the resistance group.

Dean's vulnerability as a half-blood made him particularly vulnerable to Snatcher persecution. His capture and escape demonstrated both his vulnerability and his resilience. His participation in the Battle of Hogwarts alongside his peers proved that even those without exceptional magical power could contribute meaningfully to the final victory.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

SEAMUS FINNIGAN

Seamus Finnigan was known for his explosive spellwork, both literally (often causing accidental magical explosions) and metaphorically through his passionate nature. His initial skepticism about Harry's warnings gave way to loyal conviction after he witnessed evidence of Voldemort's return. His hot temperament and loyalty made him a valuable if unpredictable member of Dumbledore's Army.

Seamus's transformation from doubter to committed resistance member reflected his honest re-evaluation of evidence. His key role in the final battle, particularly his destruction of Voldemort's Death Eater supporters, demonstrated that even those prone to magical mishaps could become effective fighters when properly trained and motivated. His survival and post-war success proved his potential beyond his Hogwarts-era reputation.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

LAVENDER BROWN

Lavender Brown was a social and emotionally expressive Gryffindor whose romantic entanglement with Ron Weasley (earning her the nickname "Lavender Vane" among students) seemed frivolous to many. Yet beneath her focus on social life lay genuine courage and commitment. Her decision to join the DA despite her initial fears demonstrated her willingness to grow beyond her comfort zone.

Lavender's grave injury during the final battle, when she was attacked by the monstrous Fenrir Greyback, marked her as another casualty of war. Her survival, though physically and psychologically traumatized, represented the long-term costs of conflict extending far beyond death. Her arc demonstrated that social personalities could harbor genuine depth and courage.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

PARVATI PATIL

Parvati Patil enjoyed Divination and social activities, yet her participation in the DA revealed a serious side concerned with practical magical defense. Her willingness to learn combat magic alongside her more traditionally social pursuits demonstrated that Gryffindor values of courage could coexist with various personality types. Her determination to fight against Voldemort's forces showed her genuine commitment beyond her social persona.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

LEE JORDAN

Lee Jordan was famous throughout Hogwarts for his humorous and irreverent Quidditch commentary that challenged broadcasting conventions. His friendship with Fred and George Weasley and his willingness to publicly support Harry Potter demonstrated genuine courage despite social pressure. His participation in the secret broadcast Potterwatch during the ministry's corruption provided crucial information to the wizarding community.

Lee's role as a broadcaster made him valuable to the resistance as a propagandist and truth-teller during a period of governmental lies and propaganda. His continuation of magical broadcasting post-war ensured that voices of resistance and truth remained active in public discourse. His survival and career flourishing represented those who used communication and journalism to fight injustice.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

COLIN CREEVEY

Colin Creevey was a young Muggle-born wizard whose uncritical adoration of Harry Potter sometimes bordered on obsessive. His enthusiasm and bravery masked his younger age and relative inexperience with magic. His rapid advancement in the DA demonstrated his serious commitment to learning despite his youthful hero-worship. His decision to sneak back into Hogwarts during the final battle, though underage, reflected his genuine desire to fight for what he believed in.

Colin's death in the Battle of Hogwarts marked the loss of youthful potential and innocent enthusiasm. His young age highlighted the moral tragedy of a war that conscripted even teenagers into its final confrontation. His death served as a reminder of the human costs of Voldemort's tyranny.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

DENNIS CREEVEY

Dennis Creevey was Colin's younger brother, equally enthusiastic about the wizarding world and his admiration for Harry Potter. His late joining of the DA reflected his need to prove himself worthy alongside his older brother. His participation in the DA alongside older students demonstrated his determination to contribute to the resistance movement despite his youth and inexperience.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

ALICIA SPINNET

Alicia Spinnet was a skilled Gryffindor Chaser whose athletic prowess and magical ability complemented her genuine commitment to defensive magic training. Her participation in the DA reflected her recognition that athletic talent needed to be balanced with combat training. Her loyalty to Hogwarts and her willingness to fight during the final battle demonstrated her priorities transcended athletic achievement.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

ANGELINA JOHNSON

Angelina Johnson was a talented and responsible Chaser who eventually assumed leadership as Gryffindor Quidditch Captain. Her willing participation in the DA reflected her understanding that team leadership required both magical combat training and moral conviction. Her willingness to lead both on the Quidditch pitch and in battle demonstrated her multifaceted capabilities.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

KATIE BELL

Katie Bell was a skilled Chaser whose promising Quidditch career was threatened by a cursed necklace placed as part of an assassination attempt. Her recovery from the dark curse injury demonstrated both her physical resilience and her psychological determination. Her return to Hogwarts to fight in the final battle despite her previous trauma showed genuine courage and commitment to her peers.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

PADMA PATIL

Padma Patil was a logical and intelligent Ravenclaw whose academic excellence reflected her house's values. Her participation in the DA represented her decision to apply her intellectual capabilities to practical magical combat. Her willingness to stand against authority, particularly through her willingness to join the resistance, demonstrated that intellectual excellence complemented moral courage.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw

MICHAEL CORNER

Michael Corner was a Ravenclaw student whose participation in the DA reflected his belief in Harry's warnings about Voldemort's return. His willingness to join the resistance despite Ravenclaw's more cautious house tendency demonstrated individual moral conviction. His support for the rebellion even under Ministry pressure showcased his commitment to justice over personal safety.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw

TERRY BOOT

Terry Boot was a Ravenclaw student who initially questioned Harry's warnings but eventually joined the DA after becoming convinced of the truth. His serious approach to training and his eventual participation in the final battle demonstrated his transformation from skeptic to committed resistance member.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw

ANTHONY GOLDSTEIN

Anthony Goldstein was a Ravenclaw student who valued both knowledge and justice, combining academic rigor with moral conviction. His enthusiastic participation in the DA reflected his understanding that intellectual knowledge must be paired with practical magical ability. His commitment to fighting against Death Eaters and their ideology demonstrated that abstract principles of justice required concrete action.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw

HANNAH ABBOTT

Hannah Abbott was a loyal and genuinely kind Hufflepuff student who embodied the values of her house. Her commitment to Dumbledore's Army reflected her understanding that friendship and loyalty had to be backed by courage and combat readiness. Her willingness to learn defensive magic and fight for her peers demonstrated that kindness did not preclude strength.

Hannah's bravery in the Battle of Hogwarts and her eventual career as a skilled Healer showed her dedication to protecting and helping others. Her post-war path combined her natural compassion with professional expertise, making her a healer in both magical and emotional senses. Her journey represented that gentleness and strength were not mutually exclusive qualities.

HOUSE: Hufflepuff

SUSAN BONES

Susan Bones was a Hufflepuff student whose family had been decimated by Death Eater violence, making her particular stake in the conflict deeply personal. Her joining of Dumbledore's Army reflected not merely academic interest but genuine motivation born of loss and a desire for justice. Her commitment to training in defensive magic transcended mere self-protection and represented her need to fight the evil that had taken her family.

Susan's hard training and brave participation in the final battle demonstrated her transformation from a grieving student into a determined warrior. Her survival allowed her the opportunity to honor her fallen relatives' memory through her continued commitment to justice and magical law. Her arc showed that personal tragedy could fuel determination rather than despair.

HOUSE: Hufflepuff

ERNIE MACMILLAN

Ernie Macmillan was proud and outspoken, sometimes to the point of being tedious with his constant invoking of his family's honor and his house's rules. Yet beneath his pomposity lay genuine ethical conviction and belief in fairness and justice. His wholehearted commitment to Dumbledore's Army showed that his pride and his principles were not merely surface characteristics but genuine values.

HOUSE: Hufflepuff
