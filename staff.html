HOGWARTS STAFF

The esteemed teachers, keepers, and healers who guide the minds and guard the gates of Hogwarts Castle.

KEEPERS OF KNOWLEDGE

Hogwarts School of Witchcraft and Wizardry relies on a faculty of exceptionally powerful and dedicated witches and wizards. Led by legendary Headmasters and professors of every discipline, they teach the next generation of magical youth while standing ready to defend the castle's ancient walls.

CHARACTERS OF THE GROUP

ALBUS DUMBLEDORE

Albus Percival Wulfric Brian Dumbledore was the Headmaster of Hogwarts. Tall, thin, and very old, he radiated power and benevolence. He founded the Order of the Phoenix and possessed the Elder Wand.

HOUSE: Gryffindor WAND: 15", Elder, Thestral Hair PATRONUS: Phoenix

MINERVA MCGONAGALL

The Transfiguration Professor and Deputy Headmistress. She is a severe-looking woman who is strict but cares deeply for her students. She is a fiercely powerful duelist and an Animagus.

HOUSE: Gryffindor WAND: 9½", Fir, Dragon Heartstring PATRONUS: Cat

SEVERUS SNAPE

The Potions Master. A polarizing figure who bullied students but possessed immense talent. He was a double agent who served Dumbledore out of an enduring, unrequited love for Lily Potter.

HOUSE: Slytherin WAND: Unknown PATRONUS: Doe

FILIUS FLITWICK

The Charms Master and Head of Ravenclaw. A tiny wizard with a squeaky voice. Despite his stature, he was a former Dueling Champion.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw WAND: Unknown ROLE: Charms Master

POMONA SPROUT

The Herbology Professor and Head of Hufflepuff. A squat witch usually covered in earth. She is cheerful, practical, and nurturing.

HOUSE: Hufflepuff WAND: Unknown ROLE: Herbology Professor

SYBILL TRELAWNEY

The Divination Professor. While many considered her a fraud due to her dramatic predictions of doom, she was a true Seer who made the critical prophecies regarding Voldemort.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw WAND: 9½", Hazel, Unicorn Hair ROLE: Seer

HORACE SLUGHORN

A Potions Master who enjoyed the comforts of life and "collecting" famous students. He possessed the critical memory of Tom Riddle asking about Horcruxes.

HOUSE: Slytherin WAND: 10¼", Cedar, Dragon Heartstring PATRONUS: Unknown

RUBEUS HAGRID

The Keeper of Keys and Grounds. A half-giant with a wild tangle of black hair. He was expelled from Hogwarts but remained Dumbledore's most loyal servant.

HOUSE: Gryffindor WAND: 16", Oak (Broken) PATRONUS: None

ARGUS FILCH

The caretaker of Hogwarts. He was a Squib, which made him bitter and resentful toward students. He was obsessed with rules and punishments.

STATUS: Squib LOYALTY: Hogwarts PET: Mrs. Norris

QUIRINUS QUIRRELL

The Defense Against the Dark Arts professor who was possessed by the weakened spirit of Lord Voldemort. He died when he attempted to touch Harry.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw WAND: 9", Alder, Unicorn Hair LOYALTY: Voldemort

GILDEROY LOCKHART

A dashingly handsome celebrity author who was a fraud. He stole stories from brave wizards and erased their memories.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw WAND: 9", Cherry, Dragon Heartstring PATRONUS: Non-corporeal

MADAM HOOCH

The Flying Instructor and Quidditch referee. She has short, spiky gray hair and hawk-like eyes. Strict about broomstick safety.

ROLE: Flying Instructor EYE COLOR: Yellow WAND: Unknown

FIRENZE

A centaur who taught Divination at Hogwarts. Unlike other centaurs, he did not distrust humans and saved Harry in the Forbidden Forest.

SPECIES: Centaur HOME: Forbidden Forest STATUS: Banished from Herd

AURORA SINISTRA

Aurora Sinistra taught Astronomy at Hogwarts. Her classes were held in the Astronomy Tower. She was calm and academic in nature. She remained loyal to Hogwarts.

SUBJECT: Astronomy

SEPTIMA VECTOR

Septima Vector was the Arithmancy professor. She specialized in magical numbers and equations. Her subject required precision and logic. She was highly intellectual.

SUBJECT: Arithmancy

BATHSHEDA BABBLING

Bathsheda Babbling taught Ancient Runes. She studied old magical languages. Her subject was complex and academic. She was respected by Ravenclaws.

SUBJECT: Ancient Runes

CHARITY BURBAGE

Charity Burbage taught Muggle Studies. She supported equality between Muggles and wizards. Her views angered Death Eaters. She was murdered by Voldemort.

SUBJECT: Muggle Studies

CUTHBERT BINNS

Professor Binns taught History of Magic. He was the only ghost teacher at Hogwarts. His lectures were extremely dull. Students struggled to stay awake.

ROLE: Teacher TYPE: Ghost SUBJECT: History of Magic

SILVANUS KETTLEBURN

Silvanus Kettleburn was a former Care of Magical Creatures professor. He lost several limbs due to dangerous creatures. His teaching methods were reckless. He was eventually replaced by Hagrid.

SUBJECT: Care of Magical Creatures

WILHELMINA GRUBBLY-PLANK

Wilhelmina Grubbly-Plank was a substitute Care of Magical Creatures teacher. She was far more practical than Hagrid. Students found her lessons safer. She was calm and professional.

SUBJECT: Care of Magical Creatures

AMYCUS CARROW

Amycus Carrow taught Dark Arts during Voldemort’s control. He promoted cruelty and punishment. Students were tortured under his rule. He was arrested after the war.

SUBJECT: Dark Arts

ALECTO CARROW

Alecto Carrow taught Muggle Studies under Voldemort. She promoted hatred toward Muggles. Her lessons were propaganda-based. She was defeated at Hogwarts.

SUBJECT: Muggle Studies

MADAM PINCE

Madam Pince was the Hogwarts librarian. She was extremely strict about book care. Students feared damaging library books. She valued silence above all.

ROLE: Librarian

POPPY POMFREY

Madam Pomfrey was the Hogwarts matron. She healed magical injuries with skill. She was firm but deeply caring. Students trusted her completely.

ROLE: Matron

OGG

Ogg was the Hogwarts gamekeeper before Hagrid. He worked under Dumbledore. Ogg was loyal to Hogwarts. He retired before Harry’s time.

ROLE: Gamekeeper
