RESIDENTS & MUGGLES

The shopkeepers, journalists, and Muggles who inhabit the broader wizarding world.

BEYOND THE CASTLE WALLS

The wizarding world extends far beyond Hogwarts. From the shops of Diagon Alley to the cozy pubs of Hogsmeade, this category captures the diverse individuals—including the Muggles who cross paths with magic—who populate the everyday life of the magical archives.

CHARACTERS OF THE GROUP

ABERFORTH DUMBLEDORE

Albus's brother and owner of the Hog's Head. Grumpy but helped the D.A. enter Hogwarts.

HOUSE: Gryffindor ROLE: Barman PATRONUS: Goat

GARRICK OLLIVANDER

The premier wandmaker. Believes "the wand chooses the wizard." Kidnapped by Voldemort.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw ROLE: Wandmaker SHOP: Diagon Alley

XENOPHILIUS LOVEGOOD

Luna's eccentric father and editor of "The Quibbler." Betrayed Harry to save Luna.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw ROLE: Editor FEATURE: Deathly Hallows Symbol

RITA SKEETER

Sensationalist reporter for the Daily Prophet. An unregistered Animagus (Beetle).

ROLE: Journalist ANIMAGUS: Beetle (Unregistered) TOOL: Quick-Quotes Quill

MADAM ROSMERTA

Madam Rosmerta owned the Three Broomsticks Inn. She was friendly and well-informed. Rosmerta was placed under the Imperius Curse. She later recovered and survived the war.

INN: Three Broomsticks

TOM (LEAKY CAULDRON)

Tom was the landlord of the Leaky Cauldron. He welcomed witches and wizards warmly. Tom knew many magical regulars. He witnessed major wizarding events.

INN: Leaky Cauldron

FLOREAN FORTESCUE

Florean Fortescue owned an ice-cream parlour. He helped Harry with history homework. Fortescue was kidnapped by Death Eaters. He was later murdered.

SHOP: Ice Cream Parlour

MR. BORGIN

Mr. Borgin co-owned Borgin and Burkes. He dealt in Dark artifacts. Borgin feared the Malfoy family. He survived by staying neutral.

SHOP: Borgin and Burkes

THE FAT LADY

The Fat Lady guarded Gryffindor Tower. She demanded passwords for entry. The portrait enjoyed singing. She fled after Sirius Black attacked her.

TYPE: Portrait

SIR CADOGAN

Sir Cadogan briefly replaced the Fat Lady. He was brave but reckless. Cadogan challenged students to duels. He was eventually removed.

TYPE: Portrait

WALBURGA BLACK

Walburga Black was Sirius’s mother. Her portrait screamed insults. She despised blood traitors. The portrait was magically silenced.

TYPE: Portrait

ARIANA DUMBLEDORE

Ariana Dumbledore was Albus’s sister. She suffered from uncontrolled magic. Ariana’s death deeply affected Albus. Her portrait remained hidden.

RELATION: Dumbledore Family

VERNON DURSLEY

Vernon Dursley was Harry’s uncle. He despised magic. Vernon treated Harry cruelly. He was forced into hiding.

STATUS: Muggle

PETUNIA DURSLEY

Petunia Dursley was Harry’s aunt. She envied her sister Lily. Petunia resented magic deeply. She reluctantly sheltered Harry.

STATUS: Muggle

DUDLEY DURSLEY

Dudley Dursley bullied Harry as a child. He feared Dementors after an attack. Dudley slowly changed his behavior. He later showed gratitude to Harry.

STATUS: Muggle

MARGE DURSLEY

Marge Dursley was Vernon’s sister. She insulted Harry’s parents. Marge was magically inflated. Her memory was later modified.

STATUS: Muggle

MR. GRANGER

Mr. Granger was Hermione’s father. He was a dentist. He supported Hermione fully. His memory was altered for safety.

STATUS: Muggle

MRS. GRANGER

Mrs. Granger was Hermione’s mother. She was also a dentist. She trusted Hermione’s decisions. Her memory was later restored.

STATUS: Muggle

FRANK BRYCE

Frank Bryce was a Muggle gardener. He worked at the Riddle House. Frank overheard Death Eaters. He was murdered by Voldemort.

STATUS: Muggle

STAN SHUNPIKE

Stan Shunpike was the conductor of the Knight Bus. He spoke with a strong accent and seemed simple-minded. Stan was wrongly imprisoned in Azkaban. He was later freed after Voldemort’s defeat.

SERVICE: Knight Bus

ERNIE PRANG

Ernie Prang was the elderly driver of the Knight Bus. He had poor eyesight. Ernie relied on Stan for navigation. Despite this, he drove magically well.

SERVICE: Knight Bus

THE SHRUNKEN HEAD

The Shrunken Head was mounted on the Knight Bus. It provided directions and commentary. The head spoke rapidly and loudly. It added chaos to every journey.

TYPE: Magical Artifact
