THE ORDER OF THE PHOENIX

The secret society founded by Albus Dumbledore to actively oppose Lord Voldemort and his Death Eaters.

LIGHT IN THE DARKNESS

Originally formed during the First Wizarding War, the Order of the Phoenix is a group of brave witches, wizards, and aurors committed to fighting Voldemort's dark regime. Operating from 12 Grimmauld Place, they risked everything in the shadows to protect the innocent and ensure the Dark Lord's downfall.

CHARACTERS OF THE GROUP

SIRIUS BLACK

Harry's Godfather. The only person to escape Azkaban unassisted. Reckless, loyal, and died dueling Bellatrix.

HOUSE: Gryffindor ANIMAGUS: Black Dog PATRONUS: Non-corporeal (Dog?)

REMUS LUPIN

A werewolf and brilliant teacher (Moony). He married Tonks and died in the final battle.

HOUSE: Gryffindor WAND: 10¼", Cypress, Unicorn Hair PATRONUS: Wolf

ALASTOR "MAD-EYE" MOODY

The greatest Auror of modern times. Heavily scarred and paranoid. Coined "Constant Vigilance!"

HOUSE: Unknown ROLE: Auror FEATURE: Magical Eye

NYMPHADORA TONKS

A clumsy, bubbly Auror and Metamorphmagus. She married Lupin and died in the final battle.

HOUSE: Hufflepuff ABILITY: Metamorphmagus PATRONUS: Jack Rabbit / Wolf

KINGSLEY SHACKLEBOLT

A high-ranking Auror with a deep voice. He protected the Muggle PM and became Minister for Magic.

ROLE: Auror / Minister WAND: Unknown PATRONUS: Lynx

ARTHUR WEASLEY

Weasley patriarch. Obsessed with Muggles. A kind father who fought in the Battle of Hogwarts.

HOUSE: Gryffindor ROLE: Ministry Official PATRONUS: Weasel

MOLLY WEASLEY

Weasley matriarch. A fierce duelist who killed Bellatrix Lestrange to protect her daughter.

HOUSE: Gryffindor ROLE: Homemaker / Order Member WAND: Unknown

MADAME OLYMPE MAXIME

Headmistress of Beauxbatons and a half-giantess. She accompanied Hagrid to negotiate with giants.

SCHOOL: Beauxbatons ANCESTRY: Half-Giant ROLE: Headmistress

BILL WEASLEY

Eldest Weasley brother and Curse Breaker. Married Fleur Delacour. Scarred by Greyback.

HOUSE: Gryffindor ROLE: Curse Breaker STATUS: Pure-blood

CHARLIE WEASLEY

Second eldest brother. A dragonologist in Romania. Returned with dragons for the final battle.

HOUSE: Gryffindor ROLE: Dragonologist WAND: 12", Ash, Unicorn Hair

JAMES POTTER

James Potter was Harry Potter’s father and a founding Order member. He was brave, talented, and fiercely loyal to his friends. James defied Voldemort three times. He was murdered while protecting his family.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

LILY POTTER

Lily Potter was a gifted witch and Harry’s mother. She was known for her kindness and intelligence. Lily sacrificed herself to save Harry. Her love protected him from Voldemort.

HOUSE: Gryffindor

FRANK LONGBOTTOM

Frank Longbottom was a respected Auror. He fought Voldemort during the First Wizarding War. Frank was tortured by Death Eaters. He was left permanently incapacitated.

ROLE: Auror

ALICE LONGBOTTOM

Alice Longbottom was Neville’s mother and a skilled Auror. She fought bravely alongside her husband. Alice was tortured with the Cruciatus Curse. She remained permanently damaged.

ROLE: Auror

MUNDUNGUS FLETCHER

Mundungus Fletcher was an unreliable Order member. He was involved in petty crime. Mundungus abandoned duties under pressure. He nonetheless provided useful information.

ROLE: Informant

ARABELLA FIGG

Arabella Figg was a Squib living near the Dursleys. She secretly watched over Harry Potter. Figg testified for Harry at his trial. She served the Order quietly.

STATUS: Squib

ELPHIAS DOGE

Elphias Doge was a lifelong friend of Dumbledore. He supported the Order loyally. Doge defended Dumbledore’s reputation. He survived both wizarding wars.

ROLE: Order Member

EMMELINE VANCE

Emmeline Vance was a dedicated Order member. She frequently guarded key locations. Emmeline was targeted by Death Eaters. She was murdered during the war.

STATUS: Deceased

STURGIS PODMORE

Sturgis Podmore guarded the Department of Mysteries. He was arrested under false charges. Podmore spent time in Azkaban. He remained loyal to the Order.

STATUS: Order Member

HESTIA JONES

Hestia Jones was a kind but firm Order member. She escorted the Dursleys to safety. Hestia supported the resistance quietly. She survived the war.

ROLE: Order Member

DEDALUS DIGGLE

Dedalus Diggle was an eccentric wizard. He celebrated Harry’s survival as a baby. Diggle helped protect the Dursleys. He was a loyal Order member.

ROLE: Order Member
