A vintage archive detailing the extraordinary tools of Magizoology, Global Magical Governance, and the artifacts of the Global Wizarding War.

THE ARCHIVES OF GOVERNANCE & DISCOVERY

Beyond the walls of Hogwarts, the international wizarding world is vast, rigidly governed, and filled with extraordinary creatures. In the early 20th century, the magical world was defined by sweeping exploration, stringent secrecy laws, and the rising threat of Gellert Grindelwald. This era of magical history is characterized by elegant bureaucratic artifacts, brass instrumentation, and the ingenious tools utilized by Magizoologists to study the world's most elusive beasts.

From the labyrinthine corridors of the British Ministry of Magic to the polished, subterranean halls of MACUSA in New York, government institutions employ highly specialized, vintage magic to maintain order, record prophecies, and conceal the wizarding world from No-Maj eyes. This archive preserves the vital instruments of discovery, secrecy, and magical law.

I. FANTASTIC BEASTS ARTIFACTS

NEWT SCAMANDER’S SUITCASE

An unassuming, battered leather briefcase that houses one of the greatest magical achievements in Magizoology. Equipped with a "Muggle-Worthy" dial to hide its contents from customs inspectors, the suitcase is enchanted with a phenomenally powerful Undetectable Extension Charm.

Inside lies a vast, multi-biome magical menagerie where Newt rehabilitates and studies endangered magical creatures. It contains an entire wooden shed that serves as a potions lab and study area, accessible via a small ladder from the suitcase opening.

OWNER: Newton Scamander

THE BLOOD PACT PENDANT

An incredibly rare, intricately carved silver vial containing a magically bound pact forged between a young Albus Dumbledore and Gellert Grindelwald. The pact was sealed with their intertwined blood and prevented them from ever fighting each other.

Any attempt to break the pact or betray the other would cause the pendant to physically attack the offender. It was ultimately stolen back from Grindelwald by Newt's Niffler, allowing Dumbledore to eventually find a way to destroy it.

CREATORS: Dumbledore & Grindelwald

GRINDELWALD’S SKULL HOOKAH

A macabre, enchanted human skull used by the dark wizard Gellert Grindelwald. When a user draws from the pipe attached to the skull, it exhales thick, swirling magical smoke that physically manifests detailed, prophetic visions.

Grindelwald famously used this artifact during his rally in the Lestrange Mausoleum beneath Paris, projecting terrifying, visceral imagery of the impending Second World War to manipulate his followers into believing that Muggles must be subjugated for the greater good.

USAGE: Dark Divination

MACUSA WAND PERMITS

In the 1920s, the Magical Congress of the United States of America (MACUSA) operated under Rappaport's Law, demanding extreme segregation and secrecy from No-Maj society. As part of this, every witch and wizard entering or residing in America was required to carry a highly regulated Wand Permit.

These elegant, official documents cataloged the wand's wood, core, length, and the owner's magical signature, ensuring the government could track any rogue magic cast within city limits.

ISSUER: Magical Congress of the USA

OBSCURUS CONTAINERS

When a young magical child is forced to suppress their magic through physical or psychological abuse, they develop an Obscurus—a dark, parasitic, destructive magical force. If the child dies, the Obscurus typically vanishes.

However, Newt Scamander managed to extract a dark, swirling Obscurus from a deceased Sudanese girl, successfully containing the volatile energy within a magically reinforced, frosted glass bubble. It was kept suspended in his suitcase lab for study.

CONTENT: Parasitic Magical Energy

THUNDERBIRD FEATHER ARTIFACTS

The Thunderbird, an enormous majestic bird native to North America, can create storms as it flies and is highly sensitive to danger. Its shimmering, iridescent feathers are potent magical components, often used by the wandmaker Shikoba Wolfe to forge powerful, difficult-to-master wands.

Newt Scamander utilized the Thunderbird Frank's innate magical properties, releasing him into the sky above New York to distribute Swooping Evil venom via a localized rainstorm, wiping the terrifying memories of the city's No-Maj population.

SOURCE: The Thunderbird

LESTRANGE FAMILY RELICS

The Lestrange family, one of the oldest and most wealthy pure-blood families, possessed numerous ancient artifacts. Among the most closely guarded was the intricate, mechanized Lestrange Family Tree housed deep within the French Ministry of Magic's Records Room.

Stored in an ornate box, the artifact unfolded into a glowing, three-dimensional representation of their twisted, tragic lineage, featuring golden vines and portraits that visually confirmed the complex, often cruel history of their bloodline.

OWNERSHIP: The Lestrange Family

II. MINISTRY OF MAGIC ARTIFACTS

WIZENGAMOT CHAINS

In the terrifying, subterranean courtrooms of the Ministry of Magic, the accused are seated in an intimidating wooden chair located in the center of the room. This chair is equipped with heavy, rusted iron chains that magically spring to life, binding the prisoner's arms and legs tightly during the trial.

The chains are cold, unyielding, and react to the presiding judge's magical commands, a stark reminder of the Ministry's supreme authority over magical law.

LOCATION: Courtroom Ten

AUROR EQUIPMENT

Aurors, the elite magical law enforcement officers tasked with tracking down Dark Wizards, utilize highly specialized gear. This includes Sneakoscopes to detect deception, Secrecy Sensors, and Foe-Glasses that show shadowy figures of enemies closing in.

Their wands are often registered and heavily utilized for combat, while their trench coats are frequently charmed with shield spells and undetectable extension charms to carry numerous investigative tools and neutralizing potions.

ISSUER: Department of Magical Law Enforcement

INTERDEPARTMENTAL MEMOS

Before the current system was implemented, Ministry departments communicated via enchanted owls, which created an unbearable mess of feathers and droppings throughout the building.

The solution was the enchanted Interdepartmental Memos. Pale violet paper airplanes charmed to fly gracefully through the corridors, riding the lifts, and seeking out their intended recipients' desks to deliver their magical messages silently and cleanly.

USAGE: Bureaucratic Communication

SELF-WRITING QUILLS

A staple of magical bureaucracy and journalism. While Dicta-Quills (like the one employed by Rita Skeeter) are enchanted to maliciously embellish and exaggerate the speaker's words, standard Self-Writing Quills are incredibly useful for official transcriptions.

Used heavily during Wizengamot trials, these quills stand upright independently, furiously scribbling verbatim records of the proceedings onto long rolls of parchment without the need for an inkpot or a human hand.

USAGE: Magical Dictation

PROBITY PROBES

Thin, golden rod-like instruments utilized primarily for magical security. When passed over a person or an object, they detect spells of concealment, hidden magical artifacts, or dark enchantments.

During the Second Wizarding War, their use became mandatory at high-security locations like Gringotts Wizarding Bank and the visitor entrances to the Ministry of Magic to prevent Death Eaters and spies from infiltrating with polyjuice potion or hidden weapons.

USAGE: Magical Security Checkpoint

GRINGOTTS VAULT KEYS

While the deepest, highest-security vaults at Gringotts require the stroke of a certified goblin's finger to open, the standard vaults utilize small, incredibly intricate golden keys.

These keys are impossible to duplicate through magical means. The goblins guard the records of ownership fiercely, and presenting a stolen key often results in the goblin employing the bank's lethal countermeasures against the thief.

ISSUER: Gringotts Wizarding Bank

WIZARDING COINS

The standard currency of the magical world, minted exclusively by goblins. It consists of three denominations: the large gold Galleon, the silver Sickle (17 to a Galleon), and the bronze Knut (29 to a Sickle).

The coins are struck with serial numbers referring to the goblin who minted them. They are enchanted to resist standard duplication spells like *Geminio*, ensuring the wizarding economy remains stable and free from counterfeiters.

CURRENCY: Galleons, Sickles, Knuts
