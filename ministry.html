MINISTRY OF MAGIC

The governing body of the British magical community, maintaining law, secrecy, and order.

LAW AND ARCHIVES

Located deep beneath London, the Ministry of Magic regulates every aspect of wizarding life. From the Department of Mysteries to the Auror Office, its officials strive to preserve the Statute of Secrecy, administer justice, and maintain order in the magical world, though not without bureaucracy and vulnerability to corruption.

CHARACTERS OF THE GROUP

CORNELIUS FUDGE

Cornelius Oswald Fudge served as Minister for Magic during a critical period in wizarding history. Preoccupied with maintaining his public image and popularity, he was fundamentally unprepared for the return of the Dark Lord. When faced with evidence of Voldemort's resurrection, Fudge's denial and inability to accept reality proved catastrophic for the wizarding world.

Characterized by his fondness for lime-green bowler hats and his reliance on Ministry bureaucrats, Fudge prioritized political stability over the truth. His refusal to acknowledge the danger allowed Voldemort to operate unchecked for a crucial year. Ultimately, Fudge's inadequacy as a leader contributed to the chaos and suffering of the Second Wizarding War, cementing his legacy as one of the Ministry's most ineffective ministers.

ROLE: Minister for Magic (former) HOUSE: Unknown FEATURE: Lime Green Bowler Hat

RUFUS SCRIMGEOUR

Rufus Scrimgeour replaced Fudge as Minister for Magic, bringing military experience and a more decisive approach to leadership. Having served as Head of the Auror Office, he was far more competent and battle-hardened than his predecessor. With his leonine appearance and scarred face, Scrimgeour commanded respect through demonstrated magical prowess.

However, Scrimgeour faced an uphill battle against an increasingly powerful Voldemort. Despite his efforts to mobilize the Ministry's defenses and coordinate with Dumbledore, he was ultimately captured by Death Eaters and tortured for information regarding Harry Potter's whereabouts. Rather than betray the boy, Scrimgeour maintained his dignity and was executed for his refusal to cooperate, dying as a principled leader willing to sacrifice everything for his principles.

ROLE: Minister for Magic BACKGROUND: Auror Office Head FATE: Deceased

PIUS THICKNESSE

Pius Thicknesse served as the final Minister during Voldemort's second rise to power, though his tenure occurred entirely under duress. Placed under the Imperius Curse by the Death Eaters, Thicknesse became a puppet minister, forced to issue decrees that systematized the persecution of Muggle-borns and consolidated Dark wizard power throughout the Ministry.

While technically occupying the highest office in the wizarding government, Thicknesse was stripped of all free will and agency. He enacted policies that transformed the Ministry into an instrument of Voldemort's oppression, including the Muggle-Born Registration Commission. After the Dark Lord's final defeat, Thicknesse was freed from the curse, though his reputation remained forever tainted by his forced association with these atrocities.

ROLE: Minister for Magic

DOLORES UMBRIDGE

Dolores Jane Umbridge represents the darkest face of bureaucratic tyranny. As Senior Undersecretary to the Minister, she was sent to Hogwarts to impose Fudge's authority and silence Harry Potter's warnings about Voldemort's return. Obsessed with maintaining order and adorned in an array of sickeningly sweet pink cardigans decorated with cat plates, she hid a sadistic cruelty beneath a saccharine exterior.

Umbridge employed blood quills to carve messages into the hands of punished students, drawing their own blood while making them write punishments repeatedly. Her introduction of an Inquisitorial Squad of student informants created an atmosphere of fear and totalitarianism within Hogwarts. Though eventually removed and humiliated, Umbridge later collaborated openly with the Death Eaters, cementing her status as one of the most despicable figures in the wizarding world.

HOUSE: Slytherin WAND: 8", Birch, Dragon Heartstring PATRONUS: Persian Cat

BARTY CROUCH SR.

Barton Crouch Senior was a high-ranking Ministry official and Head of the Department of International Magical Cooperation. Renowned for his stern demeanor and unwavering commitment to law and order, he was a rigid enforcer of justice who brooked no exceptions. His uncompromising nature made him both respected and feared throughout the Ministry.

Crouch's personal tragedy mirrored his political failures: his own son, Barty Crouch Jr., became a Death Eater and was imprisoned in Azkaban. When Crouch Jr. escaped and sought revenge, he murdered his father, leaving the distinguished official dead and his reputation in ruins. The incident exposed the fundamental weakness in Crouch's approach—his inability to show mercy even to his own family.

ROLE: Ministry Official HOUSE: Unknown FATE: Deceased

LUDO BAGMAN

Ludo Bagman was a charismatic, youthful-looking Ministry official who served as Head of the Department of Magical Games and Sports. A former international Quidditch Beater of considerable talent, Bagman brought enthusiasm and popularity to his position. His outgoing personality and jovial manner made him beloved by many in the wizarding community.

However, Bagman's charming exterior concealed a serious gambling addiction that left him deeply indebted to unsavory individuals, including goblins. His compulsive wagering and resulting financial troubles eventually forced him to flee the country to escape his creditors. His career in the Ministry unraveled when his debts became public knowledge, transforming him from a celebrated official into a cautionary tale of how personal vice can destroy even the most promising careers.

ROLE: Ministry Official FORMER JOB: Beater HOUSE: Unknown

PERCY WEASLEY

Percy Ignatius Weasley is the ambitious and studious third Weasley brother who pursued a career in the Ministry of Magic with singleminded determination. Characterized by his rigid adherence to rules and protocols, he served as an Assistant to the Minister, believing that loyalty to the Ministry's bureaucratic structure superseded family bonds.

During the Ministry's denial of Voldemort's return, Percy chose to support Fudge over his own family's warnings, creating a painful rift that lasted until the Battle of Hogwarts. His decision to prioritize Ministry politics over family loyalty haunted him profoundly. Recognizing his error too late, Percy eventually reconciled with his family and fought bravely in the final battle, his redemption serving as a reminder that ambition untempered by love and loyalty can lead even good people astray.

HOUSE: Gryffindor ROLE: Assistant to Minister STATUS: Pure-blood

AMOS DIGGORY

Amos Diggory was a dedicated Ministry official in the Department for the Regulation of Magical Creatures, known for his solid character and pride in his son, Cedric Diggory. A man of conviction and integrity, Amos took genuine pride in Cedric's achievements as a talented Seeker and an exemplary student. He represented the best of Hufflepuff values—hard work, loyalty, and fairness.

The death of Cedric at the hands of Peter Pettigrew shattered Amos profoundly. The loss of his son transformed him, the vibrant pride in his voice replaced by the hollow tones of unrelenting grief. Amos became a symbol of the collateral damage of war—a good man destroyed by the violence of forces beyond his control, destined to carry the weight of his son's untimely death for the rest of his days.

DEPARTMENT: Magical Creatures

BRODERICK BODE

Broderick Bode was a Ministry official assigned to the mysterious and secretive Department of Mysteries. His work with classified magical research placed him at the intersection of sensitive state secrets and Dark magic. When Bode fell under the control of the Imperius Curse through Death Eater manipulation, he became an unwitting tool of Voldemort's agents.

Bode's eventual hospitalization at St. Mungo's Hospital for Magical Maladies and Injuries provided little sanctuary. Death Eaters, determined to silence him, placed a cursed plant in his hospital room that slowly poisoned him to death. His demise exemplifies how even those working far from the front lines of conflict could fall victim to the war's machinations.

DEPARTMENT: Department of Mysteries

MAFALDA HOPKIRK

Mafalda Hopkirk was an anonymous functionary within the Ministry's Improper Use of Magic Office, tasked with tracking and punishing underage wizards who broke the magical law outside of school. Through numerous form letters bearing her name, she became a symbol of the Ministry's bureaucratic machinery—impersonal, efficient, and seemingly unstoppable.

Though Hopkirk never appeared in person, her stern letters warning of expulsion and punishment terrified countless young witches and wizards. She represented the faceless authority of a system designed to maintain order, yet often blind to context and circumstance. Her role epitomized the ways that large institutions can make individuals culpable for unjust systems through their unwitting participation.

OFFICE: Improper Use of Magic

REGINALD CATTERMOLE

Reginald Cattermole was an ordinary Ministry functionary caught in the machinery of Voldemort's persecution. Falsely accused of being Muggle-born by the corrupted Ministry authorities, he faced trial before the kangaroo-court Muggle-Born Registration Commission. His case exemplifies the injustice of totalitarian systems built on false accusations and predetermined guilt.

When Harry Potter impersonated Cattermole using Polyjuice Potion to infiltrate the Ministry, he inadvertently gave Reginald the opportunity to escape. Reginald and his wife Mary fled with their children, seeking refuge away from a Ministry consumed by Dark wizard corruption. Their fate demonstrates how ordinary citizens become collateral damage in larger conflicts.

STATUS: Ministry Worker

MARY CATTERMOLE

Mary Cattermole, Reginald's devoted wife, faced trial before the Muggle-Born Registration Commission on the false charge of Muggle ancestry. Her desperate pleas for mercy—not for herself but for her children's safety and future—expose the cruelty of Voldemort's regime. Standing before a tribunal determined to condemn her, Mary embodied the terror experienced by thousands accused without evidence.

Harry Potter's brave intervention helped Mary escape this unjust proceeding, though her ordeal exemplified the daily horrors inflicted by the corrupted Ministry. Her case demonstrates that behind every bureaucratic atrocity are real people with families, hopes, and the fundamental dignity that totalitarian regimes systematically strip away.

STATUS: Muggle-born Witch

ALBERT RUNCORN

Albert Runcorn held significant authority within the Ministry as a high-ranking official who became closely allied with anti-Muggle-born policies. His position of power made him a key figure in Voldemort's apparatus of persecution, and his name became synonymous with the systematic oppression of Muggle-borns during the Dark regime.

When Harry Potter assumed Runcorn's appearance using Polyjuice Potion, he discovered the considerable influence that Runcorn's position commanded. Using this authority, Harry was able to free prisoners and subvert Death Eater operations within the Ministry itself. Runcorn's identity became an ironic instrument of his own regime's downfall, demonstrating that those who wield power in unjust systems can inadvertently enable their own opposition.

STATUS: Ministry Official
