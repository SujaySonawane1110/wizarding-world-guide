HORCRUXES & DARK ARTIFACTS

Cursed objects, fragments of the soul, and artifacts steeped in dark magic. Some relics are better left forgotten.

THE TOLL OF DARK MAGIC

Dark magic leaves an indelible mark on both the caster and the world around them. Among the most sinister creations are Horcruxes—objects containing a torn fragment of a wizard's soul, anchoring them to life as long as the object remains intact. Creating even a single Horcrux requires a supreme act of evil, tearing the soul apart through the act of murder.

Beyond Horcruxes, the wizarding world is plagued by countless cursed artifacts. From objects meant to maim or mind-control, to those that cause slow and agonizing torment, these relics serve as a grim reminder of magic's corrupting potential when twisted toward malicious ends.

I. THE HORCRUXES

TOM RIDDLE’S DIARY

Created by a sixteen-year-old Tom Riddle, this seemingly innocuous blank diary was his first Horcrux, forged after the murder of Moaning Myrtle. It held not only a fragment of his soul but also his memories of unlocking the Chamber of Secrets.

Unlike other Horcruxes, the diary was designed as a weapon as much as a safeguard, capable of manipulating its possessor. It slowly drained the life force of whoever confided in it, allowing the memory of Riddle to manifest in the physical world.

CREATOR: Lord Voldemort

MARVOLO GAUNT’S RING

An heirloom of the Gaunt family, descendants of Salazar Slytherin, this ring was set with the Resurrection Stone—one of the three Deathly Hallows. Voldemort, unaware of the stone's true nature, turned the ring into his second Horcrux.

To protect it, Voldemort placed a deadly, flesh-decaying curse upon the ring. When Albus Dumbledore later discovered it, the temptation to see his lost family overwhelmed him, causing him to don the ring and trigger the curse that would ultimately spell his doom.

CREATOR: Lord Voldemort

SALAZAR SLYTHERIN’S LOCKET

A heavy gold locket bearing the serpentine 'S' of Salazar Slytherin. Passed down to Merope Gaunt, it was sold for a pittance before Voldemort reclaimed it by murdering Hepzibah Smith.

Once turned into a Horcrux, the locket was hidden in a treacherous seaside cave, protected by blood magic and an army of Inferi. The locket actively fought against its destruction, tormenting the mind of anyone who wore it with their darkest fears and insecurities.

CREATOR: Lord Voldemort

HELGA HUFFLEPUFF’S CUP

A beautifully wrought golden cup with two handles, originally belonging to Hogwarts founder Helga Hufflepuff. Voldemort stole it from Hepzibah Smith, transforming a symbol of nourishment and hospitality into a dark anchor for his soul.

He entrusted the cup to Bellatrix Lestrange, who secured it deep within the Gringotts wizarding bank in the Lestrange family vault. The vault was protected by extreme enchantments, including the Gemino and Flagrante curses, which caused objects to multiply and burn upon touch.

CREATOR: Lord Voldemort

ROWENA RAVENCLAW’S DIADEM

Said to grant wisdom to its wearer, this delicate sapphire-studded tiara was stolen by Ravenclaw's daughter, Helena, and hidden in the forests of Albania. Centuries later, Tom Riddle charmed Helena's ghost into revealing its location.

Believing himself to be the only one to fully uncover the secrets of Hogwarts, Voldemort arrogantly hid the diadem in the Room of Requirement. It remained there for decades among centuries of hidden student contraband.

CREATOR: Lord Voldemort

NAGINI

A massive, venomous Maledictus snake, Nagini was Voldemort's loyal companion and his final intentional Horcrux. Made with the murder of Bertha Jorkins, creating a living Horcrux was a highly unusual and dangerous choice.

Because Nagini possessed a piece of Voldemort's soul, the Dark Lord had unprecedented control over her, often sending her to spy or execute his enemies. Her venom was exceptionally deadly, keeping wounds open and preventing them from healing.

CREATOR: Lord Voldemort

HARRY POTTER

The unintended Horcrux. When Voldemort's Killing Curse rebounded off infant Harry, the Dark Lord's already mutilated soul fractured once more. The broken fragment latched onto the only living thing in the room: Harry himself.

This soul fragment granted Harry the ability to speak Parseltongue and forged a deep, psychic connection into Voldemort's mind. To truly defeat the Dark Lord, this accidental Horcrux had to be destroyed by Voldemort's own hand.

CREATOR: Accidental Creation

II. DARK ARTIFACTS

HAND OF GLORY

A gruesome artifact often favored by thieves and dark wizards. The Hand of Glory is a shriveled human hand, which, when a candle is inserted between its fingers, provides light only to the person holding it.

The light it casts is impervious to magical darkness, such as Peruvian Instant Darkness Powder, allowing its wielder to navigate and commit crimes unseen by others. Draco Malfoy utilized one purchased from Borgin and Burkes to guide Death Eaters through Hogwarts.

ASSOCIATION: Borgin and Burkes

CURSED NECKLACE

An opulent opal necklace renowned for carrying a devastating, lethal curse. It was noted to have claimed the lives of nineteen Muggle owners before ending up in the display cases of Borgin and Burkes.

Merely brushing against the necklace causes instant, violent magical trauma, suspending the victim in mid-air in agonizing pain. Katie Bell narrowly survived an encounter with the necklace only because she touched it through a tiny tear in her glove.

ASSOCIATION: Dark Curses

BLOOD QUILLS

A sinister instrument of torture masquerading as a writing implement. The Black Quill does not require ink; instead, as the user writes, it magically slices into the back of their hand, drawing blood to form the words on the parchment.

Repeated use scars the user permanently. Dolores Umbridge utilized this cruel artifact during detentions at Hogwarts, forcing students to carve dictations into their own flesh as a barbaric method of "discipline."

ASSOCIATION: Dolores Umbridge

INFERI & NECROMANCY

Inferi are dead bodies reanimated by a Dark Wizard's complex necromantic magic. They are not alive, merely puppets commanded to do the bidding of their master. Their creation is considered one of the darkest branches of magic.

They feel no pain and are relentlessly single-minded. As creatures of darkness and death, they fear light and warmth, making fire the only effective weapon against them. Voldemort used an army of Inferi to guard his Horcrux locket.

ASSOCIATION: Necromancy

BASILISK FANG

While naturally occurring, the fang of a Basilisk is imbued with one of the most potent and destructive venoms in the wizarding world. Its venom is so incredibly toxic that it has only one known antidote: phoenix tears.

Due to this unparalleled destructive capability, Basilisk venom is one of the few substances capable of damaging a Horcrux beyond magical repair. Harry Potter used a fang to destroy Tom Riddle's diary in the Chamber of Secrets.

ASSOCIATION: Chamber of Secrets

THE DARK MARK

Not a physical object, but a deeply cursed brand seared into the left forearm of Voldemort's inner circle of Death Eaters. It depicts a skull with a serpent protruding from its mouth like a tongue.

Through the Protean Charm, Voldemort could touch any Death Eater's mark to summon his followers, causing their marks to burn black. Conversely, followers could summon him. It was a terrifying symbol of loyalty and dark servitude.

ASSOCIATION: Death Eaters

BORGIN AND BURKES RELICS

The shadowy shop in Knockturn Alley is a haven for the darkest magical artifacts. Its inventory boasts items of malevolent intent, from human bones and instruments of torture to cursed jewelry.

Among its infamous stock is the Vanishing Cabinet, which Draco Malfoy used to smuggle Death Eaters into Hogwarts, and a deck of blood-stained playing cards. The shop caters explicitly to Dark Wizards seeking power or malice.

ASSOCIATION: Knockturn Alley
