DEATH EATERS

The fanatical followers of Lord Voldemort, dedicated to pure-blood supremacy and dark magic.

THE SHADOW OF THE DARK LORD

Bound by the Dark Mark, the Death Eaters are a group of dark wizards who serve Lord Voldemort. Terrorizing the wizarding world with curses, manipulation, and death, they seek to overthrow the Ministry of Magic, subjugate Muggles, and enforce pure-blood supremacy.

CHARACTERS OF THE GROUP

LORD VOLDEMORT

The Dark Lord. Psychopathic and obsessed with power and immortality via Horcruxes.

HOUSE: Slytherin WAND: 13½", Yew, Phoenix Feather PATRONUS: None

BELLATRIX LESTRANGE

Voldemort's most loyal lieutenant. Sadistic and unhinged. She was fanatically devoted to the Dark Lord.

HOUSE: Slytherin WAND: 12¾", Walnut, Dragon Heartstring PATRONUS: None

LUCIUS MALFOY

Head of the Malfoy family. Wealthy and slippery. Fell from grace after failing to get the Prophecy.

HOUSE: Slytherin WAND: 18", Elm, Dragon Heartstring PATRONUS: None

NARCISSA MALFOY

Draco's mother. Her love for her son superseded her loyalty to Voldemort, leading her to lie to the Dark Lord.

HOUSE: Slytherin WAND: Unknown LOYALTY: Malfoy Family

BARTY CROUCH JR.

Fanatical Death Eater who impersonated Mad-Eye Moody to deliver Harry to Voldemort.

HOUSE: Ravenclaw FATE: Dementor's Kiss LOYALTY: Voldemort

REGULUS BLACK

Sirius's brother. Defected from Death Eaters and sacrificed himself to steal the Locket Horcrux.

HOUSE: Slytherin ROLE: Seeker (Slytherin) FATE: Deceased

PETER PETTIGREW

Betrayed the Potters to Voldemort. Spent twelve years as a rat (Scabbers). Resurrected Voldemort.

HOUSE: Gryffindor WAND: 9¼", Chestnut, Dragon Heartstring ANIMAGUS: Rat

FENRIR GREYBACK

Savage werewolf who attacked children. Mercenary for the Death Eaters.

SPECIES: Werewolf LOYALTY: Voldemort (Mercenary) WAND: Unknown

ANTONIN DOLOHOV

Antonin Dolohov was a dangerous Death Eater. He used powerful silent curses. Dolohov fought in both wizarding wars. He was defeated at Hogwarts.

STATUS: Death Eater

YAXLEY

Yaxley was a high-ranking Death Eater. He infiltrated the Ministry of Magic. Yaxley enforced anti-Muggle-born laws. He was defeated during the war.

STATUS: Death Eater

AUGUSTUS ROOKWOOD

Rookwood was a Death Eater spy. He worked in the Department of Mysteries. Rookwood passed secrets to Voldemort. He was later imprisoned.

ROLE: Spy

RODOLPHUS LESTRANGE

Rodolphus Lestrange was Bellatrix’s husband. He helped torture the Longbottoms. Rodolphus was loyal to Voldemort. He was imprisoned in Azkaban.

STATUS: Death Eater

RABASTAN LESTRANGE

Rabastan Lestrange was Rodolphus’s brother. He fought alongside Bellatrix. Rabastan participated in torture crimes. He was imprisoned in Azkaban.

STATUS: Death Eater

SCABIOR

Scabior was a leader among the Snatchers. He hunted Muggle-borns for rewards. Scabior captured Harry and his friends. He was later overpowered.

GROUP: Snatchers

IGOR KARKAROFF

Igor Karkaroff was a former Death Eater. He betrayed others to avoid Azkaban. Karkaroff became Headmaster of Durmstrang. He was murdered after fleeing Voldemort.

SCHOOL: Durmstrang

EVAN ROSIER

Evan Rosier was an early Death Eater. He fought against Aurors. Rosier was killed during the First Wizarding War. Moody lost part of his nose fighting him.

STATUS: Deceased

WILKES

Wilkes was a Death Eater during the first war. He participated in violent attacks. Wilkes was killed by Aurors. His death weakened Voldemort’s forces.

STATUS: Deceased
